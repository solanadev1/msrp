The window sticker for Solana coins. Paste a coin address. MSRP reads the blockchain and tells you who made the coin, who funded the people who made it, what else they've launched, and how much of the supply is really held by strangers.

Free. No wallet, no sign-up. Every number links to the transaction it came from.

Tip: press / to jump here. Pasting an address checks it straight away.
